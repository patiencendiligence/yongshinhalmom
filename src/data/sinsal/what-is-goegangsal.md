# 괴강살(魁罡殺)이란 무엇인가?

괴강살은 최고와 최강을 뜻하는 강렬한 신살이라네.

'우두머리 괴(魁)'와 '북두칠성 강(罡)'이 합쳐진 이름처럼, 극단적으로 크고 강한 기운을 담고 있지.

사주에 괴강살이 있으면 잘되면 크게 잘되고, 안되면 크게 흔들리는 극과 극의 인생을 살기 쉽다네.
<!-- /ko -->
<!-- en -->
# What is Goegang-sal (Supreme Command Star)?

Goegang-sal is an intense special star representing the very top and the very strongest.

Combining characters meaning "chief" and "the pole star of the Big Dipper," its name carries an extreme, powerful energy.

Having Goegang-sal in one's chart often means a life of extremes—soaring high when things go well, and shaken hard when they don't.

## 괴강살의 핵심 의미

괴강살의 본질은 '평범함을 거부하는 힘'이라네.

작은 성공보다 크게 성취하려는 야망이 강하고, 남들과 같은 길보다 자기만의 길을 개척하려 하지.

이 힘을 잘 다스리면 큰 인물이 되지만, 다스리지 못하면 극단으로 치우치기 쉽다네.
<!-- /ko -->
<!-- en -->
## Core Meaning of Goegang-sal

The essence of Goegang-sal is a force that resists the ordinary.

Ambition leans toward major achievement rather than small wins, and there's a strong drive to forge one's own path rather than follow the crowd.

Mastered well, this force can produce a truly great figure; left unmastered, it can easily swing to extremes.

## Traits of a Strong Goegang-sal

* Strong charisma and presence
* Thinks in big pictures and acts boldly
* Strong competitive drive and pride
* Sometimes makes extreme choices
* Naturally ends up in leadership positions

They show their true worth on a big stage, not a small one.
